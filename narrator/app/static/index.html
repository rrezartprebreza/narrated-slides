    Quick guide

    1. Add a deck. Drop a .pptx on the home page, or press Try the sample deck.
    2. Write the script. Whatever is in a slide's speaker notes is what gets spoken. Edit it in the box under the slide. Changes save automatically.
    3. Hear it. Preview voice plays the current slide's notes. Slides with no notes stay silent for a few seconds.
    4. Export. Choose a voice and speed, then Create narrated video. Download the MP4 when it finishes.
    5. Want to change a slide's design? Edit the deck in PowerPoint or Keynote, upload it again, and your video is rebuilt in minutes. Deck with notes in the export step gives you the file with your final notes inside.
